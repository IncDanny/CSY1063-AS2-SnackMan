How to Play:

Controls:

Move with W, A, S, D keys, Arrow Keys, or On-Screen Buttons

Objective:

Collect all points to advance to the next level!

Characters:

You — navigate the maze and collect points!

Enemy — avoid them or lose a life!

Stunned Enemy — you can eat them for points!

Power-Ups:

Extra Life — Gain +1 life

Speed Boost — Move faster!

Gone Wild! — Enemies become stunned and you can eat them for 5 points each!

Levels:

As you progress, expect either more or faster enemies!
